## Steps

1. Turn side-on to the target.
2. Lift the knee across the body, foot flexed.
3. Pivot the standing foot so the heel points at the target.
4. Thrust the leg out straight, striking with the foot blade (balnal) or heel.
5. Retract to the chamber before landing.
