## Steps

1. Start in a fighting or front stance, hands up.
2. Raise the knee of the kicking leg until the thigh points at the target.
3. Extend the lower leg sharply, pulling the toes back.
4. Strike with the ball of the foot (apchuk).
5. Snap the foot back to the chamber.
6. Set the foot down in front, ready for the next technique.
