## Steps

1. Start in a fighting stance.
2. Raise the knee of the back leg towards the target.
3. Pivot on the standing foot and turn the hip over.
4. Extend the leg and strike with the instep (baldeung) or ball of the foot.
5. Retract along the same line and land in a fighting stance.
