commonMistakes: ["Pulling straight back against all four fingers", "Using arm strength alone", "Not following up or moving away"]

## Steps

1. Open your hand and keep the elbow down.
2. Turn your wrist towards the gap between their thumb and fingers.
3. Drive your elbow forwards towards their wrist as you step in.
4. Pull free through the thumb and move to a guard.
